Our Challenges

We are proud of how far Antonia's has come, but honesty matters just as much as pride. Here is what we are up against — and why your support can change a girl's story.

Not Enough Tools & Equipment

We stretch what little we have across many eager hands — hairdressing tools like dryers, clippers, combs and wig stands; manicure & pedicure sets like foot spas and nail kits; and facial/beauty materials like steamers and skincare kits. Every extra tool means one more girl gets a real turn to practice.

One Small Room, A Growing Family

Antonia's currently runs from a single room. Every term more girls join us wanting to learn, and the space gets more congested — we simply need a bigger home for our training.

Getting Our Girls To Their Clients

Our students are often taken out to serve clients directly — bridal work, door-to-door services. Reliable transport is a constant hustle, and sometimes the money for it just isn't there, yet we never want to turn away a girl who is ready to work and learn.

Bursaries That Run Out Fast

We give bursaries to girls who cannot pay in full — single mothers, orphans, school dropouts, girls abandoned by partners — but our bursary fund is small compared to how many need it.

Room To Grow, Beyond Masaka

With more support we could open a bigger training centre and reach more girls across Uganda and East Africa — not just Kimanya. Every contribution moves us closer to that.

If any of this speaks to you, you can contribute towards solving these challenges and help us widen our skilling centre — even internationally.

Transforming Beauty into Self-Reliance

Located in Kimanya - Masaka, Uganda, East Africa, Antonia's Learning and Beauty Centre is dedicated to delivering world-class hair, makeup, and body care services while empowering the community.

Our mission focuses heavily on equipping the girl child with practical, income-generating skills. Through our bursary schemes and flexible budget programs, every ambitious student can learn, build self-reliance, and launch a successful career in beauty and entrepreneurship.

Whether you visit us for a glam makeup transformation, bridal styling, or to study salon management, we guarantee elegance, professionalism, and warm hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and vocational skilling program

How soon can a student start earning income?

At Antonia's Learning & Beauty Centre, our practical curriculum is structured for rapid skill acquisition:

  • Intensive Hands-on Training: Students get real practical practice from day one on hairdressing, makeup, and nail care.
  • Earning within 2 Months: Within just 60 days of enrollment, students gain sufficient proficiency to serve paid clients or start working under guidance while continuing advanced entrepreneurship and salon management modules.
  • Pay-After-Gaining-Skills Option: We also facilitate flexible payment plans where students can pay tuition balance after they begin earning.
Are bursaries available for skilling young women?

Yes, bursaries are a core part of our empowerment mission for the girl child:

  • Financial Support & Bursaries: We offer dedicated bursaries to vulnerable and ambitious students to reduce tuition burdens.
  • Flexible Budget Learning: Anyone who desires to learn can be taught according to their individual budget.
  • Complete Entrepreneurship Package: Tuition includes practical training, customer care, and salon management skills for total independence.
Do I need to book in advance for bridal services?

For bridal styling (Tukola Ku Bagole), advance booking is recommended to guarantee quality service:

  • Timely Reservations: We advise booking 1 to 2 weeks in advance to allow time for bridal trials, theme matching, and scheduling.
  • Full Package Inclusions: Our bridal team provides hair styling, high-definition makeup, matching jewelry sets, and bridesmaid styling.
  • On-Site or Mobile Service: We cater to brides both at our salon in Kimanya - Masaka and directly at event venues upon request.
